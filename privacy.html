Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Privacy Summary

Imposter+ is built with a privacy-first design: no accounts, no remote server tracking, no advertising SDKs, and local on-device gameplay. Diagnostic error reporting is 100% voluntary, giving you complete visibility and control over any data you choose to send.

Imposter+ is an independent mobile application developed by Kamal Neupane ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application ("App") on iOS and iPadOS. We believe your party games and private gatherings should remain private.

1. Privacy by Design Architecture

Imposter+ operates almost entirely on your physical device. We do not maintain user accounts, do not require registration or passwords, do not run remote player databases, and do not track your activity across other applications or websites.

2. Information We Process & Collect

The App clearly distinguishes between information stored locally on your device (which is not automatically transmitted to us) and information you voluntarily choose to transmit to us via email:

A. On-Device Storage & Custom Content (Stored Locally)

Your game preferences, theme selections, player avatar choices, accessibility settings (such as Dyslexic Font and Reduce Motion), and custom prompt categories created using the Category Creator or Prompt Pack managers are stored solely on your device using local device storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This data remains on your physical device, is never automatically transmitted to us or third parties, and is only shared if you explicitly choose to export a file using the iOS share sheet.

B. Motion & Sensor Data (Processed in Memory)

When playing Forehead Frenzy, the App accesses real-time accelerometer and device tilt data solely to detect nod-down (correct) and tilt-up (pass) gameplay gestures. Motion data is processed strictly in temporary volatile memory during active rounds and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Handled by Apple)

If you choose to purchase an Imposter+ Premium subscription or lifetime unlock, the transaction is billed and processed directly by Apple Inc. through the App Store using StoreKit APIs. We do not collect, receive, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses. Apple validates transactions and provides the App with cryptographic receipt and transaction status information locally on your device to unlock Premium features. All purchase data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

D. Voluntary Diagnostic Error Reporting (Sent via Email)

The App includes an optional, user-controlled Error Reporting feature to help us investigate, reproduce, and resolve unexpected errors, crashes, or software defects. When you choose to compile an error report, the App generates a diagnostic summary that may include:

  • Error Details: Error code (e.g., GAME_LOAD_042), error message, error category/type, occurrence count in the current session, and timestamp.
  • Application Information: App version, build number, subscription status (Free or Premium), session duration, and active screen/game mode.
  • Device Technical State: Device model (e.g., iPhone 17 Pro), iOS version, battery percentage, charging state, available disk storage, screen orientation, and system language/locale.
  • Network Diagnostic State: General connection type (Wi-Fi, Cellular, or Offline) and internet availability status.
  • Recent Session Activity: A rolling log of the last 50 high-level user actions/taps within the app leading up to the error (e.g., "Opened Game Library", "Selected Game: Mafia").
  • Optional User Notes: Any optional description, context, or reproduction steps you choose to type into the report form.

Your Control Over Diagnostic Data: Before an error report is sent, you are presented with an interactive preview screen where you can inspect all diagnostic details and individually toggle any section on or off (the Error Code is the only required field). You have complete visibility and control over what is included.

Email Transmission & Receipt of Email Address: Error reports are generated within the App and handed off to your device's native mail composer (such as Apple Mail or your chosen email app). The report is sent as an email through your own email provider to our support address (imposter-plus@outlook.com). Consequently, we necessarily receive your sender email address along with the diagnostic report and any text or attachments you include in the message.

Strict Purpose Limitation: We use your email address and diagnostic report solely to investigate, troubleshoot, reproduce, and fix the reported technical issue, and to reply to you with assistance or resolution details if necessary. We do not sell, rent, or intentionally disclose your email address to third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

E. Direct Support & Feedback Communications

If you contact us directly by email for customer support, bug reporting, or general feedback, we receive your email address and message contents to respond to your inquiry and support the App.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure absolute clarity regarding your privacy, Imposter+ does not:

  • Collect precise or approximate GPS location data;
  • Access your personal contacts, address book, microphone, or photo library;
  • Include third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or analytics tracking frameworks;
  • Collect Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking; or
  • Use tracking cookies, web beacons, or user fingerprinting technologies.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (Under GDPR & International Laws)

For users in jurisdictions requiring a lawful basis for data processing (such as the European Economic Area under the GDPR and the United Kingdom under the UK GDPR), we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): To investigate user-initiated error reports, diagnose technical defects, maintain application security and stability, and respond to support inquiries.
  • Performance of a Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): To deliver the core gameplay experience and validate in-app purchase entitlements.
  • Legal Obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): Where processing or retaining records is required to comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

5. Overseas Data Disclosure

When you send an email or diagnostic error report to our support address (imposter-plus@outlook.com), your communication is processed by Microsoft as our email service provider. Depending on the services and infrastructure involved, your email and related information may be transferred to, stored, or processed outside Australia or your country of residence. Microsoft handles this information in accordance with its applicable privacy and security practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical measures to protect your information. On-device data is secured within iOS's sandboxed application architecture. Email communications may be protected in transit using encryption such as TLS, depending on the email providers and services involved.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On-Device Data: Game preferences, themes, and custom categories remain on your device until you delete them using the in-app "Delete All Data" button in App Settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Support & Error Report Emails: Emails and diagnostic reports sent to our support inbox are generally retained for up to 90 days following the resolution of the reported issue or inquiry, after which we will take reasonable steps to securely delete the information, subject to any applicable legal, security, backup, or service-provider retention requirements. In limited circumstances, communications may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for ongoing technical investigations into recurring defects, dispute resolution, or to satisfy legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Purchase Records: Transaction histories are managed and retained directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's privacy policy.

8. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics platforms. The only third-party services involved in connection with the App are:

  • Apple Inc.: Provides App Store distribution, payment and subscription processing via StoreKit, and native iOS system frameworks under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple does not receive ordinary gameplay data from the App.
  • Microsoft: Email service provider used to receive and reply to user-initiated support requests and error reports sent to imposter-plus@outlook.com in accordance with Microsoft's email infrastructure and security practices. Microsoft does not receive ordinary gameplay data from the App.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have specific legal rights regarding your personal information, which apply where applicable under local data protection laws:

  • Access & Portability: The right to request a copy of any personal communications or data we hold about you.
  • Rectification & Erasure: The right to request that we correct inaccurate data or delete your support emails, subject to applicable retention requirements. You can also erase all on-device data instantly within the App.
  • Restriction & Objection: The right to object to or request the restriction of our processing of your personal information.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Where any processing is based on your consent, the right to withdraw that consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal).
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

These rights apply where applicable under regional privacy frameworks, including the GDPR & UK GDPR (EEA and United Kingdom), the California Consumer Privacy Act / CCPA / CPRA (California), the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Canada's PIPEDA, and Brazil's LGPD. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

10. Privacy Complaints Process

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints about how we handle your personal information, please contact Kamal Neupane directly at imposter-plus@outlook.com. We will acknowledge and investigate your complaint within a reasonable timeframe and communicate the outcome to you.

If you are not satisfied with our response to your complaint, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with your local privacy or data protection regulator:

  • Australia: You may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) online at www.oaic.gov.au, by email at enquiries@oaic.gov.au, or by phone at 1300 363 992.
  • European Union / United Kingdom: You may contact your national Data Protection Authority (DPA) or the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK at ico.org.uk.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where applicable law prohibits such collection without appropriate consent. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us at imposter-plus@outlook.com and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect App updates or legal developments. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
